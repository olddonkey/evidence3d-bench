# Site survey notes

- Subject: Monumental masonry building with a five-storey arched central block, lower wings, front colonnades and hipped tile roofs
- Date of visit: 9 April 2025
- Surveyor: M. T.

## Measured dimensions

| Measurement | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Overall length, front face of the central block to the rear face of the corner blocks | 70.41 m |
| Overall width across the wings | 55.47 m |
| Width of the five-storey central block | 28.04 m |
| Projection of the central block in front of the wings | 5.79 m |
| Height to the eaves (underside of the overhang) of the central block | 20.07 m |
| Height to the eaves (underside of the overhang) of the wings and ranges | 14.90 m |
| Height to the ridge of the central block roof | 25.57 m |

Values in metres are rounded to the nearest 10 mm. Heights are above the paving at the foot of the entrance steps.

## Remarks

- External survey from the public plaza only; the rear of the building, which joins a later building, was not reached.
- Lengths taken with a laser distance meter; roof heights by reflectorless total station.
